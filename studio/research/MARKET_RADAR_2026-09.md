# Market radar — September 2026

## Product thesis
The opportunity is not another workout logger. AI Fitness OS should connect five loops in one product:
1. strength programming/logging;
2. nutrition;
3. wearable recovery/readiness;
4. camera-based technique/body progress;
5. an AI explanation/orchestration layer that never replaces deterministic facts.

## Current signals

### FACT — strength interoperability is growing

Source: https://www.whoop.com/us/en/press-center/strava-overhauls-strength-experience-with-expanded-partner-ecosystem-new-workout-log-and-muscle-maps/

## Differentiation hypothesis
A strong wedge for AI Fitness OS is **wearable-neutral recovery that directly changes a deterministic strength plan, while technique CV and nutrition share the same user context**. Most competitors are strongest in one or two of these loops.

## Ranked R&D backlog
1. Real Xiaomi device calibration + morning sync reliability.
2. Recovery explanation timeline: “what changed readiness today, versus yesterday and versus my 28-day baseline?”
3. Passive/background Health Connect refresh with last-success diagnostics.
4. Robust baseline outlier handling after enough real wearable history exists.
5. iOS HealthKit provider behind the same normalized health interface.
6. Direct vendor integrations only when Health Connect/HealthKit cannot expose a valuable metric users actually need.

## Guardrail
Do not chase every wearable API. Prefer platform aggregators first, then add direct integrations based on measured user demand and missing data value.
